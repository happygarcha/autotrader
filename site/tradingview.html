Bot overview1s / 5s / 15s / 30s charts: open our terminal

TradingView's free widget has no seconds timeframes (only our terminal does). This is TradingView's own free chart widget, so prices, markets and tools come from TradingView and follow its terms. It cannot show our bot's trades or practice orders, and the bot does not read data from it. Some exchanges' stock and index data is delayed. Use the symbol box inside the chart to search any market. Our terminal is the place for second-level charts, the bot overlay and practice trading.
